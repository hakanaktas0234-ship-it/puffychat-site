Privacy Policy

This policy explains which data is processed when you use Puffy, on what legal basis, who it is shared with and how long it is kept. Instead of general statements, we list the fields we actually store.

The company acts as data controller under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and, for users in the European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

2. Personal data we process

Account and profile

When you sign up we collect your email address or phone number, depending on the sign-in method you choose (with Google sign-in, the email address of your Google account). Your profile holds: nickname, user number, profile photo and, if any, profile video, cover image, life photos, short bio, gender, date of birth, country and preferred language. You can hide your age and country from other users; when hidden, these fields are not sent to others.

Content and activity

Messages you send, room chats, moments and short videos you share, comments, likes, follows and friendships, rooms you enter, seats you take, photos and videos you share in rooms and private messages, gifts you send, and a log of voice/video calls (with whom, when and for how long) are recorded. The content of voice chats and calls is not recorded; audio and video are only transmitted in real time.

Location

Your location is only collected when you tap the send-location option in a private message: your current location is sent to the other person with that message and stored with it. The app does not collect GPS location in the background.

For security and fraud prevention, an approximate location (country/city) is derived from your connection IP address. It is determined when you open the app and is not a precise location or address. The raw IP address is not stored in this record; only a masked form (for example 85.105.*.*) is kept, visible only to authorised administrators for security reviews.

Device and technical data

Device identifier, device name and platform, push token used for notifications, IP address, last-seen time, app version and crash logs.

Payment data

Coin purchases are made through Google Play; your card details never reach us. We store the purchase record and receipt token, your wallet balance and coin transactions.

Identity verification and withdrawals

Only users who want to withdraw earnings from the platform are asked, as required by law, for identity details: full name, date of birth, address and national ID number. The ID number is stored encrypted in the database. IBAN and payment receipts are also stored. This information is used only for payment and tax obligations and is never shown to other users.

4. Legal bases

Your data is processed on the basis of: performance of a contract (providing the service), compliance with legal obligations (tax and retention duties), legitimate interests (security of the service, preventing abuse), and your explicit consent where required by law (KVKK Article 5; GDPR Article 6).

5. Service providers and international transfers

We use a limited number of service providers to run the service:

  • Supabase (AWS Frankfurt, Germany) — database, file storage and session management. Your data is stored in the EU region.
  • ZEGOCLOUD (Singapore) — real-time transmission of voice chat. The audio stream passes through these servers and is not recorded.
  • Google (Firebase) — notifications, crash reports and usage statistics.
  • Google Play — in-app purchases and receipt verification.
  • IP geolocation service — deriving an approximate country/city from the connection IP. Only the IP address is sent to the service.
  • Competent authorities — where required by law or court order.

For transfers outside Türkiye and the EEA, the safeguards required by KVKK Article 9 and GDPR Chapter V are applied.

6. Analytics and cookies

Puffy is a mobile app and does not use web cookies. The app uses Firebase Analytics (usage statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) to find bugs and improve the app; they are not used for ad targeting and are not shared with ad networks.

7. Retention

Your data is kept while your account is open. When you delete your account, a 14-day undo period starts; at the end of it your personal and social data is permanently deleted. See Account deletion for details.

8. Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 and the GDPR you have the right to: learn whether your data is processed and request information about it; learn the purpose of processing; know to whom it is transferred in Türkiye or abroad; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; request erasure; request that those corrections or erasures be notified to recipients; object to a result against you arising from automated analysis; obtain your data in a portable format (GDPR); restrict or object to processing (GDPR); withdraw consent at any time; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

You can edit most profile information directly in the app and delete your account in the app or via the account deletion page. For other requests write to destek@puffychat.com; requests are handled within 30 days at the latest. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or, if you are in the EEA, with your local data protection authority.

9. Child safety

Puffy is only for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. We have zero tolerance for child exploitation content; see our Child Safety Standards.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. The database is protected by row-level access rules; a user can only access the rows they are authorised to see. Sensitive fields such as the national ID number are additionally stored encrypted.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We announce significant changes in the app; the date at the top of this page always shows the version in force.
